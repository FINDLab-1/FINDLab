FIND-Lab: A Factor-Controlled Benchmark for Informed Navigation Diagnosis in Constrained Spaces

Anonymous Authors

Controlled factors→ Outcomes→ Termination locations→ Behavioral evidence→ Parameter proposals and re-evaluation

FIND-Lab varies interpretable factors across five constrained passage environments, changing one factor at a time: passage width in Straight; bend in Single-Bend and S-Bend, with passage width fixed at 1.2 m; initial heading in Recovery Pocket; and angular spacing or curved radius in U-Shape.

Each run connects its outcome and termination location to time series of motion, clearance, and route alignment before termination. An LLM assistant uses these diagnostic records and navigation settings to propose parameter or reward changes for re-evaluation. The records support investigation and testing; they do not establish failure causes or guarantee improvement.

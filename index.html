FIND-Lab: A Factor-Controlled Benchmark for Informed Navigation Diagnosis in Constrained Spaces

Anonymous Authors for IEEE ICRA 2027 submission

Video

Abstract

Autonomous mobile robots are increasingly deployed in constrained spaces, such as narrow aisles and tight corners, where navigation often fails. Aggregate metrics in existing benchmarks mainly indicate whether a run fails, offering limited guidance on what to inspect or adjust. We present FIND-Lab, a factor-controlled benchmark for informed navigation diagnosis in constrained spaces. We first build five environment kinds in Isaac Sim, varying interpretable factors such as passage width and bend while holding other conditions fixed, so that every outcome is tied to the setting that produced it. Next, FIND-Lab shows where failed runs terminate and links these locations to behavioral time series of motion, clearance, and route alignment to investigate why. Finally, a large language model (LLM) uses these records and settings to suggest how to adjust planner parameters or policy rewards for re-evaluation.

Notably, evaluations of four navigation methods across 33 factor settings on a wheeled robot (Nova Carter) and a legged robot (ANYmal-C) locate failure boundaries set by a configured margin rather than an impassable width, at a 0.30 m clearance penalty and at a 0.55 m obstacle envelope. In addition, among failures ending at similar locations, the records distinguish stalling with remaining clearance from continued motion until clearance is lost. Furthermore, across 29 Nova Carter conditions with reference success rates < 50%, proposals informed by added diagnostics raise pooled success from 12.11% to 22.11% for reinforcement learning with proximal policy optimization over 19 conditions, from 1.25% to 18.75% for model predictive path integral control over 8, and from 30% to 40% for the dynamic window approach over 2. We conclude that tying each outcome to its factor setting, termination location, and preceding behavior turns a failure count into evidence for what to inspect and adjust.

Research Overview

The overview links controlled environments to recorded evidence and re-evaluation. The hero visualizes separately recorded episodes together.

  1. Configure

    Control factors across five environment kinds, two robot platforms, and four navigation methods.

  2. Measure

    Record run outcomes and termination locations at each factor setting.

  3. Inspect

    Follow route progress, actual and progress speeds, centerline offset, heading error, and body, head, and tail clearance with a synchronized time cursor.

  4. Propose & test

    Compare aggregate and added diagnostic inputs, propose at most one permitted adjustment, and evaluate it separately.

Evaluation scope

These are descriptive, in-sample results on selected conditions. Evaluation sets differ by method, so comparisons should stay within each method. Identical effective A/L proposals share executions. Gains over historical F do not isolate a diagnostic-input effect for RL-PPO. Because the historical F results were used to select these conditions, gains over F may include regression to the mean. Pooled rates do not establish improvement in every condition or statistical significance.
